Good to know before you trust it 💡

  • Trained on a small public dataset; may not generalize to other scanners, hospitals, or populations.
  • Similar-looking conditions are easy to confuse (for example pneumonia, COVID-19, tuberculosis, and pulmonary fibrosis), and real patients can have more than one condition at once. This demo predicts a single label.
  • Small findings such as early lung nodules are easy to miss, and a normal-looking result never rules out disease. An X-ray can raise suspicion of lung cancer or COPD, but cannot confirm either.
  • Heatmaps show where the model looked, not proof of why it decided. Use them as a debugging aid.
  • Confidence is calibrated on the test set only and can be overconfident on unfamiliar images.
  • Unsupported images (photos, screenshots, other modalities) are flagged, but the check is imperfect.
  • Segmentation overlays are approximate and not clinically validated.
